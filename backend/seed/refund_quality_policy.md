# Order Accuracy & Food Quality Policy — v1

## Section 1 — Scope
This policy governs customer-facing refunds and credits for order accuracy
and food quality issues, separate from the delivery SLA and compensation
rules covered in the SLA & Compensation Policy. It applies to every order
placed through Swiggy or Zomato for this restaurant.

## Section 2 — Missing or Incorrect Items
### 2.1 Missing Items
If a customer reports an item missing from their order and the claim is
verified against the kitchen's packing checklist, the customer is refunded
the price of the missing item in full. No partial-order refund is issued
for a single missing item unless it represents more than 50% of the order
value.

### 2.2 Wrong Item Substituted
If an item unavailable at order time was substituted without the
customer's prior consent, the customer may request either a refund of the
price difference (if the substitute was cheaper) or store credit equal to
120% of the price difference (if the substitute was more expensive), as
an apology for the unrequested swap.

## Section 3 — Food Quality Complaints
### 3.1 Verified Quality Issues
A food quality complaint (wrong temperature, spoiled ingredients, foreign
object, or an item that does not match its menu description) verified by
photo evidence from the customer is eligible for a full refund of that
item's price plus a 15% credit toward the customer's next order.

### 3.2 Unverified Quality Complaints
A quality complaint without photo evidence or other verifiable detail is
handled case-by-case by support staff and is not guaranteed a refund under
this policy — repeated unverified complaints from the same customer within
a 30-day window should be flagged for manual review rather than
auto-approved.

## Section 4 — Kitchen-Side Cancellations
### 4.1 Item Unavailable After Order Placed
If an order is cancelled because an item became unavailable after the
order was already placed and paid for, the customer receives a full refund
of the entire order plus a 10% credit toward their next order, issued
within 24 hours of the cancellation.

### 4.2 Kitchen Overload
If an order is cancelled due to the kitchen being unable to fulfil it
within a reasonable time (kitchen overload during a rush period), the
customer receives a full refund of the order. This is distinct from a
restaurant-caused SLA delay under the SLA & Compensation Policy's §4.4,
which applies when an order is delayed but still fulfilled, not cancelled
outright.

## Section 5 — Exclusions
A complaint raised more than 48 hours after delivery is not eligible for a
refund under this policy, regardless of category, except for food-safety
complaints (Section 3.1), which may be raised within 7 days given the
delayed onset of some food-safety symptoms. Refunds under this policy are
independent of, and do not reduce, any compensation owed under the SLA &
Compensation Policy for the same order if both apply.

## Section 6 — Repeat-Offense Handling
If the same menu item generates 3 or more verified quality complaints
(Section 3.1) within a rolling 14-day window, it should be flagged to
kitchen management for review — this policy does not prescribe pulling the
item from the menu automatically, only that it be reviewed.
