# SLA & Compensation Policy — v4 (Draft Revision)

## Section 1 — Scope
This policy governs delivery SLA targets and compensation eligibility for
orders placed through Swiggy and Zomato for this restaurant. It supersedes
v3 once adopted.

## Section 2 — Delivery SLA Target
The standard delivery SLA target is 35 minutes (2100 seconds) from order
placement to delivery, measured per order — tightened from the 40-minute
target in v3 to better match what customers on both platforms now expect
during peak hours. An order is in breach if actual delivery time exceeds
this target.

## Section 3 — Cancellation Handling
Cancellations are categorized by cause: weather delay, courier no-show,
restaurant-caused delay, or customer-initiated pre-dispatch cancellation.
Only the first three categories are eligible for review under Section 4.

## Section 4 — Compensation Eligibility

### 4.1 General
Compensation is assessed per order against the categories below. An order
may only be claimed once.

### 4.2 Weather & Force Majeure
Orders delayed beyond the SLA target due to verified weather conditions or
force majeure events (including severe rain, flooding, or regional courier
disruption) are eligible for full compensation of the delivery fee plus 65%
of order value, provided the delay exceeds 10 minutes past the SLA target —
both the delay threshold (down from 15 minutes) and the payout share (up
from 50%) are more generous than v3, reflecting how disruptive weather
delays have proven to be for repeat-order trust.

### 4.3 Courier No-Show
Orders where the assigned courier fails to arrive for pickup within 25
minutes of assignment (up from 20 minutes in v3, to reduce false positives
from a courier running slightly behind rather than genuinely not showing),
resulting in cancellation or significant delay, are eligible for full
compensation of order value.

### 4.4 Restaurant-Caused Delay
Delays caused by the restaurant itself (kitchen backlog, early closure)
that breach the SLA by more than 8 minutes (down from 10) are eligible for
partial compensation — 35% of order value (up from 25%), in recognition
that these delays are the restaurant's own responsibility to control and
should be compensated more generously than delays outside its control.

### 4.5 Exclusions
Orders cancelled by the customer before dispatch are NOT eligible for
compensation under any clause in this section, regardless of stated reason.
Orders delivered within the SLA target, even if the customer later
complains, are not eligible for compensation under this section.

## Section 5 — Claim Filing
Eligible claims must reference the order ID, the applicable clause, and the
computed compensation amount. Claims must be filed within 21 days of the
order date (extended from 14 days in v3, giving operators more time to
review a backlog of drafted claims before the window closes).
