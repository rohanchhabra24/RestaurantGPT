# Delivery Delay and Cancellation Compensation Policy — v2

*Effective 10 October 2026. Supersedes all earlier versions.*

## Section 1 — Scope

This policy covers delays and cancellations on delivery orders placed
through Swiggy and Zomato at our Punjabi dhaba-style restaurant in Mumbai.
It applies to orders placed on or after the effective date and does not
cover dine-in, takeaway or phone orders. Food quality, missing items and
wrong items are handled under our separate Food Quality and Refund Policy.
Swiggy and Zomato apply their own refund rules to customers first; this
policy sets out the extra goodwill compensation the restaurant pays in the
three cases below. We may update it, and changes apply only to orders
placed after the update.

## Section 2 — Definitions and Timing Rules

### 2.1 Promised ETA
The ETA is the delivery time shown to the customer when the order was
confirmed. Later revisions to the ETA made by the platform are ignored.
All delays are measured against the original ETA.

### 2.2 Order value
Order value is the food total after restaurant discounts and before taxes,
packaging, delivery fees and platform fees. All amounts are in Indian
rupees (₹).

### 2.3 Quoted preparation time
The kitchen quotes 20 minutes for orders of up to 4 items and 30 minutes
for orders of 5 or more items, counted from the moment we accept the
order. We must accept every order within 3 minutes of it arriving.

### 2.4 Source of timestamps
Disputed times are taken from the platform order log (accepted, marked
ready, courier arrived, picked up, delivered). If the log and our kitchen
record disagree, the platform log is used.

## Section 3 — Compensation Categories

Compensation is paid only for the three categories below. A delay of
exactly the stated threshold does not qualify. It must be longer.

### 3.1 weather_delay
- Definition: A delay caused by verified weather or force majeure, meaning
  an IMD orange or red alert for Mumbai on the order date.
- Trigger: Delivery arrives more than 30 minutes after the promised ETA.
- Payout: 10% of order value, up to ₹100.

### 3.2 courier_no_show
- Definition: The courier assigned by the platform never arrives to
  collect a ready order.
- Trigger: No courier has arrived 25 minutes after the order is marked
  ready.
- Payout: Flat ₹120.

### 3.3 restaurant_closed_early / kitchen-caused delay
- Definition: The kitchen closes early after accepting an order, or the
  food is not ready on time.
- Trigger: The restaurant cancels the order after accepting it, or the
  food is ready more than 20 minutes after the quoted preparation time.
- Payout: 25% of order value, up to ₹250.

## Section 4 — Payout Rules

### 4.1 One payout per order
If more than one category applies, only the highest single payout is
paid. Payouts are never added together.

### 4.2 Maximum payout
No order receives more than ₹250 in compensation.

### 4.3 How and when payment is made
Approved payouts are sent through the platform's refund or coupon tool
where available, otherwise by UPI. UPI and wallet payouts arrive within 3
working days of approval. Card refunds arrive within 7 working days.

## Section 5 — Exclusions

### 5.1 Always excluded
- customer_cancelled_predispatch: Any order the customer cancels before
  the courier picks it up is ALWAYS excluded, whatever the reason.

### 5.2 Other exclusions
- Delays shorter than the thresholds in Section 3.
- Weather delays on days with no IMD orange or red alert for Mumbai.
- Orders with a value below ₹150.
- Delays caused by an incorrect or incomplete delivery address.
- Customer unreachable for more than 10 minutes at the delivery address,
  or order refused by the customer.
- Delays after the courier has handed the food to the customer.
- Orders already refunded in full by Swiggy or Zomato.
- A second claim for an order that has already been paid out or rejected.

### 5.3 Repeat claims
If one customer files more than 3 claims in any 30 days, each further
claim gets a manual review before payment. The review takes up to 5
working days.

## Section 6 — Filing a Claim or Complaint

### 6.1 Filing window
Claims must be filed within 7 days of the order date. Day 1 is the day
after the order. Claims filed on day 8 or later are rejected.

### 6.2 What to include
Each claim must include the platform name, order ID, order date and time,
the category from Section 3, and a screenshot of the order status or
delay message. Weather claims must also include the IMD alert for that
date.

### 6.3 Where to file
Contact the outlet manager first. If the claim is not resolved within 3
working days, escalate it to the owner.

### 6.4 Review and appeal
Every claim is answered within 2 working days of filing. A rejected claim
can be appealed once, within 3 days of the rejection, and the final
decision is given within 5 working days.
